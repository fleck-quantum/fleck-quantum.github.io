Multiwave Technologies

Multiwave Technologies is a deep-tech company pioneering AI-powered, ultra-low-field MRI to make medical imaging accessible, affordable and mobile. Combining expertise in metamaterials, physics and artificial intelligence, we collaborate with leading researchers and clinicians to advance medical imaging. Our portable MRI innovations aim to enable earlier diagnosis, preventive care and healthier aging, contributing to longevity and improved quality of life. Driven by science and meaningful societal impact, we are committed to bringing MRI to everyone, everywhere. https://multiwave.ch
